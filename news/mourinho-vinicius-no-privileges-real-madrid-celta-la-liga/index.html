Mourinho Tells Vinícius "No Privileges" as He Refuses to Guarantee Him a Place Against Celta

By Decide Football · 2026-11-21

José Mourinho will not promise Vinícius Júnior a place against Celta Vigo. "Vinícius' place doesn't matter. Starting line-up, the bench, the stands. It doesn't matter," the Real Madrid head coach said on Friday, as Managing Madrid posted on X. The La Liga game is at the Bernabéu on Saturday at 9pm CEST.

"Not by decree"

In the club's own press conference report, Mourinho went further: "Here, we don't have players with privileges. We don't have players who have to play by decree. We have a group of people working for the team, for Real Madrid."

He also called the attention on Vinícius "too much". He noted that, a few weeks ago, people were treating the forward as Real's biggest problem, and that after his goals against Singapore and Japan "we can't stop talking about Vinícius". He added: "he is an incredible player, we love him, we need him."

A squad, not an eleven

The message goes beyond one player. "He's a substitute today, a starter tomorrow, and then a substitute again and then not even a substitute because other players are arriving," Mourinho said, warning that he is close to having to "leave players in the stands". He added: "I don't want eleven good players; I want many." He said Friday was "the first day we have all the players together to train" before a run of three matches in a week.

What to watch

Whether Vinícius starts at the Bernabéu will be the first sign of how literally Mourinho meant it. Mourinho called Celta "much better than the points they have".
