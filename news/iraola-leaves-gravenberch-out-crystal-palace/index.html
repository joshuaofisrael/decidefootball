Iraola leaves Gravenberch out of Liverpool's squad at Crystal Palace

By Decide Football · 2026-11-08

Andoni Iraola has left Ryan Gravenberch out of Liverpool's matchday squad for Sunday's Premier League game at Crystal Palace.

Liverpool posted the team on X on 8 November 2026. The starting XI listed in the club's graphic is Alisson, Van Dijk, Jacquet, Kerkez, Wirtz, Szoboszlai, Isak, Mac Allister, Gakpo, Barcola and Araujo. The substitutes are Mamardashvili, Gomez, Endo, Tsimikas, Munoz, Nyoni, McConnell, Koumas and Ngumoha. Gravenberch is in neither list.

The decision in focus

On Friday Iraola had made Gravenberch's availability a question of pain tolerance. The club quoted him saying:

“Let's see if he can deal with the pain and if he can help us.”
Liverpool FC (@LFC), 6 November 2026

Liverpool's club write-up said the midfielder had a heel injury and was the only selection doubt. Sunday's team sheet answers that question: Gravenberch does not start and is not on the bench. Liverpool have not said, in the team post, whether he failed a late fitness test or was rested as a precaution.

How Decide Football reads it

Iraola had set out a simple rule on Friday: if Gravenberch could manage the pain, he would be involved. Leaving him out of the 20 entirely suggests the head coach decided the risk was not worth it for this Premier League fixture, with Mac Allister and Szoboszlai in the midfield he did pick. Any fuller explanation should come from Iraola's post-match comments.
