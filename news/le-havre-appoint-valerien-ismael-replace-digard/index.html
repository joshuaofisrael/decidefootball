Le Havre appoint Valérien Ismaël until the end of the season after sacking Didier Digard

By Decide Football · 2026-11-13

Le Havre have named Valérien Ismaël as their new head coach until the end of the season, replacing Didier Digard. The club announced the appointment on Thursday, and Ligue 1's official site says it will be the 51-year-old's first coaching job in France.

Why Le Havre made the change

The decision was driven by results. Le Figaro reports that Le Havre are bottom of Ligue 1 with just two points and no wins. The club's statement says the search was run by the president and sporting director with support from experts across Blue Crow Sports Group, the club's owners, and that his arrival is timed to coincide with the first team's return to training.

Le Figaro adds that the club's statement stressed Ismaël's experience of urgent situations, which it suggested could be very useful in the weeks ahead.

Who is Ismaël?

A former centre-back who started at Strasbourg and won the German title with both Werder Bremen and Bayern Munich, Ismaël has coached since 2014. He was most recently in charge of Blackburn Rovers in the EFL Championship, Ligue 1 says, and has also managed Barnsley, West Bromwich Albion, Watford and Beşiktaş. The club says he will be assisted by Dennis Baraznowski and Mo Sahli, with Charles Cieslinsky as goalkeeping coach.

First test

Le Havre's first match under Ismaël is away at Troyes on Friday 20 November, a meeting between the bottom two, according to Le Figaro. Strasbourg, Lens and Le Mans follow before the winter break. With a deal only to the end of the season, Ismaël has been hired for one job: keeping Le Havre in Ligue 1.
