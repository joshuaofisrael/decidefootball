Carrick's Shea Lacey Substitution Turns Manchester United's Champions League Comeback Against Roma

By Decide Football · 2026-11-04

Michael Carrick's decision to bring on 19-year-old Shea Lacey for Amad in the 75th minute helped turn Manchester United's Champions League match against Roma. With United 2-1 down at Old Trafford on Tuesday night, the academy graduate came on and, according to the BBC, set up Matheus Cunha's 86th-minute winner in a 3-2 victory that ended a nine-game winless run.

“Shea Lacey, the 19-year-old academy graduate, could be in line for first Premier League start on Sunday after game-changing cameo in Champions League win over Roma”
— Times Sport (@TimesSport), 4 November 2026

The situation when Carrick made the change

Roma had been 2-0 up inside 17 minutes. Matías Soulé scored in the 11th minute and Donyell Malen converted a penalty six minutes later. Bruno Fernandes pulled one back from the penalty spot in the 58th minute, but United were still a goal behind when Carrick sent Lacey on for Amad with a quarter of an hour left, Sky Sports reported.

Cunha equalised in the 83rd minute and scored again three minutes later. The BBC credits Lacey with the assist for the second. Cunha was then sent off for a second yellow card in the first minute of stoppage time, and United held on with ten men.

What Carrick said

Carrick praised the teenager after the match, in comments reported by the BBC:

“Delighted for Shea. Love him to bits, he's so talented, he's desperate to do well. It's such a good pick out, Matheus takes it on his back foot. We moved Matheus inside second half and put Bryan wide left to give us better balance... he was even defending at right-back at the end!”

So the Lacey change came alongside a wider second-half reshuffle. Carrick moved Cunha inside in the second half and put Bryan wide left, which he said gave the side better balance. Lacey's arrival then added a fresh attacking option for the final 15 minutes.

Carrick has also faced questions this week over leaving Fernandes on the bench at the start, a call the captain has since publicly backed after the Roma comeback. Taken together, the two decisions show a manager willing to make big selection calls in a game United could not afford to lose.

Will Lacey start on Sunday?

According to Times Sport, Lacey could be in line for his first Premier League start on Sunday. That is a report, not a confirmed team selection, and Carrick has not named his side.

The case for him is simple: he came on with United losing and helped create the winning goal. The question for Carrick is whether one impact cameo is enough to hand a 19-year-old a league start, or whether Lacey's best use for now is the role he played against Roma, coming off the bench to change a game late on. The team sheet on Sunday will give the answer.
