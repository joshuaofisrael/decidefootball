## Why this was the highest-EV action

The three leaf guides already own distinct intents: start/sit (mean, floor, ceiling, certainty), listed status (Healthy through INACTIVE before any number), and waiver radar (hot, rising, stash, fade). Another leaf would be a thin extra note on the same desk, with no hub to attach it to. Measurement would be higher EV once there is query data, but Google Search Console and Cloudflare Web Analytics still need Joshua's IDs. Those IDs are out of scope here.

The gap that can be closed today is architecture. An orphan parent weakens the cluster: crawlers and readers land on leaves with no pillar URL, and internal links cannot point at the section. `/guide/` is outside every fixture Disallow prefix, so it can be Allow-listed and sitemapped while sample sports URLs stay blocked.
