Egypt sack Hossam Hassan and hand Ali Maher the national team job

By Decide Football · 2026-11-07

Egypt have sacked head coach Hossam Hassan and his staff and appointed Ali Maher to lead the national team, the Egyptian Football Association (EFA) announced on Saturday. It is a federation decision taken days before Egypt's Africa Cup of Nations qualifiers, part of the international calendar.

Arab News Sport reported the change on X:

"Egypt parts ways with coach Hossam Hassan, with Ali Maher named as his replacement, says @EFA"

What the federation said

In its statement, carried in English by CAF, the EFA board said it had reviewed "all the circumstances and events that have unfolded recently, along with their negative impact on the national team's positive image and stability" before deciding to part ways with Hassan and his technical and administrative staff.

The board thanked Hassan for his work and asked Maher to present his plan for a new coaching staff. The EFA did not set out in detail which events it meant.

Why the decision stands out

Hassan, Egypt's record goalscorer, had been in charge since February 2024. He led the Pharaohs to fourth place at the 2025 Africa Cup of Nations and to the round of 16 at the 2026 World Cup, CAF noted. Reuters, via CNA, reported that he had renewed his contract until 2030 after the World Cup and leaves after 38 matches: 21 wins, 10 draws and seven defeats.

Reuters also pointed to recent friction. Hassan's decision to substitute Mohamed Salah in the 60th minute of a 0-0 home draw with Angola in September came under scrutiny, and Hassan was given a three-match FIFA ban for criticising the referee after the World Cup defeat by Argentina.

What comes next

Maher, 52, leaves Ceramica Cleopatra to take over. Egypt play Malawi twice this month in Africa Cup of Nations qualifying, so the new head coach's first decisions on selection and staff will come almost immediately.
