Allegri Rules Out Punitive Ritiro After Porto Thrash Napoli 4-1 in the Champions League

By Decide Football · 2026-11-05

Massimiliano Allegri has ruled out, for now, taking Napoli into a punitive ritiro, a closed training retreat, after their 4-1 Champions League defeat by Porto at the Estádio do Dragão on Wednesday night. Instead he defended his players and pointed to set pieces and tiredness. It was Napoli's seventh defeat in 14 games this season, Corriere dello Sport reported.

The table after four games

Francesco Farioli's Porto left Napoli 27th in the Champions League league phase, with three points from four matches. It was their third defeat in four European games, Il Mattino noted. Their remaining fixtures are away at Manchester City, then Club Brugge, Sabah and Viking. Porto's first and third goals both came from corners, ANSA reported.

What Allegri said

Speaking to Prime Video after the match, Allegri said (translated from Italian):

“There have been games where I could have got angry, but today we played a good game for 70 minutes. … I'm certainly disappointed, bitter, because it's a bad moment. But tonight I have nothing to reproach the players for, for the dedication and the desire they showed.”
— Massimiliano Allegri, via ANSA (translated)

He said the set pieces had to be looked at: “We need to analyse the dead-ball situations, because that's where we conceded. These are details that make the difference” (translated, via ANSA).

He also blamed fatigue and a short squad. “You play every three days, at 70 minutes I couldn't make changes,” he said, adding that there was “a bit of tiredness from playing 70 minutes on an open pitch” (translated, via Corriere dello Sport).

Why no ritiro

Asked about strong decisions, Allegri said (translated, via Corriere dello Sport):

“No, not the ritiro… We've been away for six days, tomorrow we have to go home. Then on Friday we'll train and on Sunday there's a very important game for the league.”

Il Mattino's version of the same answer ends with the team training on Friday “and we'll decide”, and ANSA describes the ritiro as ruled out “for the moment”. So the door is not fully shut if results keep going the wrong way.

Under Pressure: Lazio next

Sunday's Serie A game is a big one. Napoli host league leaders Lazio, coached by Gennaro Gattuso, at the Maradona on Sunday morning. After that comes the international break, and Allegri said he expects to get some injured players back after it.

On social media, some Napoli fans called for Allegri to go after the defeat. But there have been no reports that the club is considering a change. Allegri has chosen trust over punishment. If Napoli lose to Lazio as well, he will be under more pressure going into the break.
