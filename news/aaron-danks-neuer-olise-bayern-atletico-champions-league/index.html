Danks Faces Neuer and Olise Calls for Bayern’s Atlético Champions League Trip

By Decide Football · 2026-11-03

Aaron Danks faces two distinct selection questions before Bayern Munich's UEFA Champions League trip to Atlético Madrid: who should start in goal and whether Michael Olise is ready for a starting role. Bavarian Football Works reports that both Olise and Manuel Neuer returned to training, but their availability for the match will depend on fitness assessments and workload. Jonas Urbig also trained, leaving the goalkeeper decision open ahead of matchday.

The uncertainty is significant because Bayern are operating without manager Vincent Kompany on the touchline. The National reports that Kompany is recovering after a collapse at the club's training ground and a diagnosis of meningeal irritation. Bayern sporting director Max Eberl said the absence would be measured in weeks rather than days. Danks therefore has responsibility for the immediate match decisions during an unusually sensitive period for the club.

Danks also needs to fill an attacking-midfield role with Ismael Saibari and Serge Gnabry unavailable, according to Bavarian Football Works. He indicated that the player chosen should make use of his own qualities rather than attempt to imitate an absent team-mate. That is a meaningful tactical distinction: replacing a name on the team sheet does not necessarily mean asking the replacement to perform the same movements or occupy every space in the same way.

The goalkeeper question is more straightforward in its options but still difficult in practice. Neuer's return to training makes him a possibility, while Urbig has also been involved. The report says Bayern planned to decide after assessing both keepers on matchday. Training participation alone is not confirmation that Neuer will start, and Danks has not been reported as announcing a final choice in the cited preview. Any article claiming a confirmed goalkeeper from those comments would overstate the evidence.

The Champions League context raises the stakes. Bayern had drawn their previous two European matches, according to The National, and Danks said finishing in the top eight of the league phase was a target because it offers direct passage to the round of 16. That ambition does not determine his selection automatically: a returning player might strengthen the side but also carry workload considerations. The decision requires balancing immediate competitiveness with readiness.

Bayern's recent results show that the side has continued to function without Kompany in the dugout. The National lists a 3-1 Bundesliga win at Freiburg, a 6-1 German Cup victory against Magdeburg and a 2-2 draw with Borussia Dortmund. Those results provide context for Danks' responsibility but do not answer whether the same selections are appropriate against Atlético. Opponent, venue and available personnel all change the calculation.

Danks has signalled that Bayern will retain an aggressive approach, seeking to play on the front foot and regain possession quickly. Bavarian Football Works also reports his recognition of Atlético's possession improvements, defensive strength and cohesion. That makes the choice of attacking midfielder especially important: Bayern need a player who can contribute to their own forward play without undermining their ability to counter-press when possession changes.

The central story is not a speculative predicted XI. It is that Danks must make a set of connected decisions about Neuer, Urbig, Olise and an attacking-midfield vacancy while Kompany remains absent. Those choices will become verifiable when the official line-up is published; until then, the manager's stated criteria and the available squad information are the sound basis for analysis.
