Bristol City board decide to keep Michael Skubala after 5-0 Norwich defeat

By Decide Football · 2026-11-12

Bristol City's board has decided to keep head coach Michael Skubala in post, despite four straight EFL Championship defeats at Ashton Gate. Chief executive Charlie Boss said in a video message on the club's channels that the board met earlier this week and agreed to give Skubala the opportunity to turn around the home form, BBC Sport reports.

The latest of those home defeats was a 5-0 loss to Norwich City last Saturday, which left City 17th. Boss said the game "was a long way short of our standards", according to the BBC, and that it had been followed by tough and honest conversations at the club.

The reasoning

Boss told the club's media team that the board would not have made the decision if it did not believe the club could get through this period together, and that Skubala knows more than anyone that the team needs to improve, Bristol Live reports. He also said the owners, board and players share supporters' frustration, after sections of the crowd called for the head coach to go during the Norwich defeat.

Skubala, 44, joined in late May after winning League One with Lincoln City, replacing interim boss Roy Hodgson, according to Bristol Live. The BBC says City have won only two of their past ten matches.

What comes next

City's next Championship match is away at second-placed Middlesbrough on Saturday 21 November, followed by home games against Wrexham and Swansea either side of a trip to West Bromwich Albion, per BBC Sport. Those two home fixtures are the clearest test of the board's decision.
