Vitor Matos agrees to leave Swansea for Strasbourg, according to Romano and Sky Sports News

By Decide Football · 2026-11-17

Swansea City head coach Vitor Matos has chosen to move to Strasbourg. Transfer reporter Fabrizio Romano posted on X on Tuesday morning that Matos "has agreed to become new RC Strasbourg head coach, deal done". Sky Sports News reported that he is set to leave Swansea after agreeing terms to become the French club's new head coach. Neither club had announced it when we published.

How it happened

The Athletic's David Ornstein first reported on Monday that Strasbourg had approached Swansea about Matos in the event of Hugo Oliveira leaving the Ligue 1 club, and that the 38-year-old was keen on the opportunity despite Swansea's efforts to keep him. The Guardian reported the same day that Swansea were increasingly resigned to losing him after he indicated he wanted the move.

Why Strasbourg want him

Strasbourg, owned by Chelsea's parent company BlueCo since 2023, are two points above the Ligue 1 relegation zone, according to The Guardian. Oliveira has won three of 10 league matches since succeeding Gary O'Neil in July. Matos is a former Liverpool academy and development coach.

What Swansea lose

Matos has Swansea in the Championship play-off places, though with one win in their last five games, The Guardian says. He is under contract until 2030, so Swansea are due significant compensation. Their next match is at home to West Brom on Saturday, followed by a trip to Middlesbrough and a south Wales derby at Cardiff.

Our view: for Matos, a Ligue 1 job inside the BlueCo network is a fast step up after a year in charge at Swansea. For Swansea, the timing is awkward, with three league games in quick succession and a play-off place to protect.
