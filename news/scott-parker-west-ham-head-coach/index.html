West Ham Appoint Scott Parker as Head Coach After Sacking Nuno

By Decide Football · 2026-11-04

West Ham United have appointed Scott Parker as head coach, a day after sacking Nuno Espírito Santo. The former Hammers captain, 46, has signed a two-and-a-half-year contract and will take charge for Wednesday evening's EFL Championship match away at Cardiff City, the club confirmed.

“West Ham United can confirm that Scott Parker has agreed a deal to become the Club’s new men’s first-team Head Coach 🤝”
— West Ham United (@WestHam), 4 November 2026

Why Parker

West Ham were relegated from the Premier League last season, and the board's aim is an immediate return. Parker's record fits the brief: he won promotion from the Championship to the Premier League with Fulham in 2020, Bournemouth in 2022 and Burnley in 2025, and was named EFL Championship Manager of the Season in 2024/25 with Burnley. Interim chief executive Karim Virani said he was "the unanimous choice of the Board".

"I've been in this division three times and I've got out of this division three times," Parker told the club's website. "This is a tough division, there is absolutely no denying it." He said consistency would be "the key word for all of us".

Where West Ham stand

Nuno left on Tuesday after back-to-back home defeats, 3-2 to Southampton and 3-1 to West Bromwich Albion, leaving the pre-season promotion favourites eighth in the Championship, six points behind leaders Middlesbrough, BBC Sport reported. The club said a "thorough review of the start to the EFL Championship season" had led to the change, according to The Independent.

A familiar face

Parker made 129 appearances for West Ham between 2007 and 2011 and was named Hammer of the Year three times in a row. First-team coaches Steve Potts, Gerard Prenderville and Billy Lepine will assist him, while his former team-mate Mark Noble will work closely with him. The club said further details of his permanent backroom staff would follow.

His first test comes straight away at Cardiff, with little time on the training ground. The bigger job is turning a stuttering campaign back into a promotion push.
