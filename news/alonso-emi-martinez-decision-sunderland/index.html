Alonso delays Emi Martinez goalkeeper call until final session before Sunderland

By Decide Football · 2026-11-07

Xabi Alonso has pushed Chelsea's Premier League goalkeeper decision on Emi Martinez to the final pre-match session before Sunderland.

The club flagged the press conference on X on 6 November 2026:

Xabi Alonso with the latest on Emi Martinez and squad fitness ahead of Sunderland.
Chelsea FC (@ChelseaFC), 6 November 2026

Chelsea's official write-up quotes Alonso saying the Blues will decide after tomorrow's last training whether Martinez is fit, while noting he is training well in protective headgear after jaw surgery. Mike Penders has started the last three matches in his absence. Alonso also said Moises Caicedo will be checked after discomfort, and confirmed Jorrel Hato, Maxence Lacroix and Joao Pedro are out.

The decision in focus

Alonso is choosing between a returning No.1 who has forced his way back ahead of schedule and a young keeper already on a three-game run. He called every positional call tough and said the final call comes after Saturday's session, then Sunday.

That is a concrete selection timeline for a Premier League away day at the Stadium of Light, not a vague fitness update.

How Decide Football reads it

Goalkeeper rotations after injury often leak early. Alonso instead set a public checkpoint: final training first, team sheet second. Until Chelsea publish the XI, the manager's stated rule is wait for Saturday's session before locking Martinez or Penders.
