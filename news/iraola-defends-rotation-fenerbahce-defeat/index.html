Iraola Defends Five Changes After Liverpool Lose 3-2 at Fenerbahçe in the Champions League

By Decide Football · 2026-11-05

Andoni Iraola has defended his decision to make five changes to his Liverpool side for Wednesday's Champions League match at Fenerbahçe, which ended in a 3-2 defeat and the loss of their 100 per cent record in the competition.

The decision

Coming off a 1-0 Premier League defeat by Arsenal, Iraola started Florian Wirtz, Alexander Isak and Cody Gakpo on the bench, bringing in Dominik Szoboszlai, Rio Ngumoha and Victor Muñoz, beIN Sports reported. Bradley Barcola led the line.

"We put in Victor and Rio, they were more fresh. We thought also that for Alex it was maybe good to play 30 minutes, not more," Iraola said, according to Liverpool's official website. He admitted Barcola "has been better when he has played outside wide".

What happened

Trey Nyoni, with his first goal for the club, and the substitute Isak twice put Liverpool ahead. Mason Greenwood and Archie Brown equalised each time, and Vedat Muriqi scored the winner in the 79th minute, This Is Anfield reported.

His defence

"We've been doing it all season and all the games we've played midweek. I think we've been changing at least four, five, six, seven players. Because of the schedule that we have, it's impossible to maintain with always the [same] starting XI," Iraola said. "I trust the squad to sustain the level. I don't think it was the main reason why we lost."

He pointed instead to set pieces: "The main reason I can find to lose the game is we conceded [from] three set-pieces." Fenerbahçe scored from a free-kick, a corner and a long throw.
