Pochettino Admits He Only Picks the Team at Man Utd, and Says He Wants More Power Later

By Decide Football · 2026-11-21

Mauricio Pochettino has drawn a clear line around his new job at Manchester United. Asked about being head coach rather than manager, he said: "I am picking the team now. Of course in the future, I would like to be involved in all the main decisions that will happen on the squad," according to Fabrizio Romano on X.

The first selection call

His first big team call is the captaincy, and he is not changing it. On Bruno Fernandes's contract, Pochettino said: "Bruno is our captain and he's a very important player," adding: "We will have time to talk about that," Romano reported. That keeps Fernandes in place before United's first Premier League game under Pochettino, at Liverpool on Sunday 22 November (4:30pm UK).

What "head coach" means

The words matter. A head coach picks the starting XI, the bench and the substitutions, while recruitment usually sits with the club's football executives. Pochettino's answer accepts that split for now, but makes no secret that he wants a bigger say in who comes in and goes out. For more on the difference, see our Premier League decisions page.

What to watch on Sunday

The first teamsheet at Anfield will show his thinking. Watch whether he keeps United's recent shape or changes it straight away, and where Fernandes plays.
