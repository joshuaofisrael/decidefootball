About Decide Football

Decide Football is a news site about one thing: the decisions made by managers and head coaches in association football around the world. We cover team selection, tactical and formation changes, substitutions, rotation, dropping and recalling players, captaincy, penalty-taker choices, the squad and transfer calls managers make and explain, and the managers under pressure.

Competitions we follow: Premier League, EFL Championship, Scottish Premiership, La Liga, Serie A, Bundesliga, Ligue 1, Eredivisie, Primeira Liga, MLS, Champions League, Europa League, Conference League, Internationals.

Every story centres on one specific decision, the reasoning given for it and what followed. Read our editorial policy to see how we report.
