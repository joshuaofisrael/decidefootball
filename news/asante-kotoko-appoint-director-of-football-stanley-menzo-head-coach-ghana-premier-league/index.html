No head coach since matchday four: Kotoko have handed the dugout to their own director of football

By Decide Football · 2026-11-20

Ghana's most famous club has stopped looking outside for a manager. "Asante Kotoko have appointed Director of Football Stanley Menzo Purl as the club's new head coach as the Porcupine Warriors seek to arrest their recent dip in form," Pulse Ghana posted on X on Thursday. The appointment takes immediate effect.

The decision

Kotoko have had no permanent head coach since Eric Tinkler resigned after the first four matches of the season. Assistant Hamza Obeng ran the team in the meantime. Rather than hire from outside, the board has handed the job to Menzo, the director of football they appointed in June, for the rest of the 2026/27 Ghana Premier League season, Graphic Online reports. He keeps his director's role too.

Mendes Diogo has been named technical adviser. Reports differ on Obeng's role: MyJoyOnline and Graphic say he returns to coaching the youth team, while Pulse Ghana says he will also join the senior set-up.

Why now

The results forced it. MyJoyOnline says Kotoko are 11th with 14 points from 11 matches and have gone four league games without a win. The board also urged supporters to avoid misconduct after matches, according to MyJoyOnline.

Who is Menzo?

The Dutch coach has worked widely. Graphic lists FC Volendam, SC Cambuur, Lierse, Ajax Cape Town and Beijing Guoan, plus the national teams of Aruba and Suriname. Combining director of football and head coach puts recruitment and team selection in one person's hands. That can make a club more joined-up, but if results do not improve, there is no one above the coach to change course.

What comes next

Kotoko face Bechem United on matchday 12. One win would not fix the season, but it would ease the pressure on a decision the board has tied closely to its own director of football. More managerial changes from around the world are on our Ghana Premier League page and the Under Pressure section.
