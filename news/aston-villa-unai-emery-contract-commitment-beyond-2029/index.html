Aston Villa and Unai Emery commit beyond 2029 as new deal talks continue

By Decide Football · 2026-11-05

Aston Villa have publicly tied Unai Emery into the next phase of the club's project after a working meeting between the head coach and the hierarchy. The Premier League club said chairman Nassef Sawiris met Emery with president of football operations Roberto Olabe and director of football operations Damian Vidagany to plan the club's long-term sporting direction.

“Aston Villa offered new contract to Unai Emery, same for Damian Vidagany and Roberto Olabe: deal moving to final stages.”
— Fabrizio Romano (@FabrizioRomano), 5 November 2026

What the club said

Villa's statement said the talks established "a clear commitment beyond 2029 for the joint development of the club under the sporting leadership of Unai Emery" and framed the aim as building an ambitious, competitive side. The club did not publish a signed contract length, salary figures or a completion date in that statement.

Where the talks stand

BBC Sport reported that Emery is close to agreeing a contract extension that would hand him even more responsibility for long-term development, with talks still ongoing about the fine details, the finances and the length of the new deal. BBC Sport said the plan is to announce the new contract once those details are agreed, and that an announcement is unlikely before the weekend. Emery was already under contract until 2029, according to that report.

Romano's update said Villa want to close the door on interest from other clubs and are very happy with Emery, with matching contract progress for Vidagany and Olabe. That reporting describes talks advancing, not a completed signature.

Context around Manchester United interest

BBC Sport noted Emery has been linked with Manchester United, who Villa play this weekend at Old Trafford, should United sack Michael Carrick. The same piece said Villa previously threatened pundit Jamie Carragher with legal action after he suggested Emery would be a good United option, then dropped the threat after an apology. Those links help explain why Villa are stressing continuity now; they do not, on their own, confirm a signed extension.

What is settled, and what is not

What is on the record today is a club commitment beyond 2029 and reporting that a longer deal is close. What is not confirmed in the cited sources is that Emery has signed the new contract, or the exact years and wages. Until Villa announce those terms, the story is a public commitment and advanced talks rather than a finished deal.
