Arsenal push Arteta for Premier League Manager of the Month after a maximum points run

By Decide Football · 2026-11-06

Mikel Arteta is on the Premier League Manager of the Month shortlist, and Arsenal moved quickly to rally support behind their head coach.

The club posted on 5 November 2026:

“Maximum Premier League points 🤌 Mikel Arteta has been nominated for the @premierleague Manager of the Month award ❤️ You know what to do, Gooners 👇”
Arsenal (@Arsenal), 5 November 2026

Why the nomination fits the decision story

Manager of the Month is an award vote, but for Decide Football it is also a public verdict on recent selection and game plans. Arsenal's own framing ties the nod to a run of maximum Premier League points, the cleanest on pitch case a club can put behind its coach.

Arteta's recent league work includes the one nil win at Anfield that took Arsenal top, a result built on an early Kai Havertz goal and a shut down that left Liverpool without a shot on target. Nominations do not lock trophies, yet they freeze a moment when the league's own monthly award machinery is recognising that spell.

What happens next

The Premier League will publish the winner through its usual channels. Until then, Arsenal's post is campaigning, not crowning. The decision angle stays on Arteta: keep the same standards in selection and structure that produced the maximum points run the club is advertising.
