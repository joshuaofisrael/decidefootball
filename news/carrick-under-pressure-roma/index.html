Carrick under pressure: the Martinez question and a nine-game winless run

By Decide Football · 2026-11-02

Michael Carrick goes into Manchester United's Champions League game with Roma under heavy pressure. Saturday's 2-0 defeat at Chelsea, in which Lisandro Martinez was sent off, extended United's winless run to nine games in all competitions. Radio Times reports United have not had such a run since 1988.

United are 17th in the Premier League, above the relegation zone only on goal difference, and have lost their last two Champions League games, to Atletico Madrid and Como, the second of them 4-1.

Radio Times reported that Carrick was given the club's backing last week, but that another defeat at Old Trafford could cost him his job. At Monday's press conference, Carrick said: “The dressing room is in a good place.”

One decision in focus is whether to keep picking Martinez. His red card ban covers domestic games only, but Sports Illustrated argued that his form this season means he “has to come out of the team”.

Roma, managed by Gian Piero Gasperini, arrive unbeaten in Serie A this season with seven wins and three draws.
