Klopp Recalls Füllkrug and Hands Mohya, 17, a First Germany Call-Up for the Nations League

By Decide Football · 2026-11-05

Jürgen Klopp has made his biggest selection calls yet as Germany head coach, recalling Niclas Füllkrug and handing a first senior call-up to 17-year-old Borussia Mönchengladbach midfielder Wael Mohya in a 24-man squad for November's UEFA Nations League matches, the DFB announced on Thursday.

The fixtures

Germany play Serbia in Belgrade on 13 November and the Netherlands at the Olympiastadion in Berlin on 16 November in Nations League Group A2.

Form over reputation

Füllkrug returns after more than a year away from the national side. He leads the Bundesliga scoring chart with nine goals and has 14 goals in 24 international appearances, Bundesliga.com reported. Nick Woltemade, now on loan at Juventus from Newcastle United, is not included.

A teenager in the squad

Mohya, who has two caps for Germany's under-21s, is one of three uncapped players in the squad, alongside Brajan Gruda and Finn Jeltsch. Jamal Musiala and Serge Gnabry are unavailable through injury.

The decision tells us that Klopp is prepared to pick on current Bundesliga form rather than past selection, and to promote young players straight from the under-21 level.
