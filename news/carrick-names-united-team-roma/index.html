Carrick names his United team for Roma with key players missing

By Decide Football · 2026-11-03

Michael Carrick has named his Manchester United team for Tuesday night's Champions League league-phase match against Roma at Old Trafford, with an 8pm UK kick-off. The club announced the side on X shortly before the game:

“Your United team for tonight's #UCL clash 👊”
— Manchester United (@ManUtd), 3 November 2026

Carrick picked from a stretched squad. Luke Shaw, Patrick Dorgu, Manuel Ugarte and Benjamin Sesko were all unavailable, and Marcus Rashford had been a doubt with a hip injury. Speaking on Monday, Carrick said Shaw “definitely won't be around this week” and, on Rashford, “we'll just have to see, day by day, how he keeps improving”.

One of the biggest calls concerned Lisandro Martinez. The defender was sent off in Saturday's 2-0 defeat at Chelsea, but his ban covers domestic games only, so he was available to Carrick in Europe.

The selection comes at a difficult moment. United went into the game winless in nine matches in all competitions and 21st in the league-phase table, having lost their last two Champions League games.
