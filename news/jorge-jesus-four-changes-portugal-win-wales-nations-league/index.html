Jorge Jesus makes four changes and Portugal beat Wales 3-2 to top their Nations League group

By Decide Football · 2026-11-18

Portugal head coach Jorge Jesus responded to Saturday's surprise 4-1 home defeat by Denmark by making four changes to his starting XI for Tuesday's Nations League Group A4 match in Cardiff, the Football Association of Wales reports. Portugal needed only a point to finish top of the group. They won 3-2.

The decision

Portugal's starting XI, in the order listed on Sky Sports' team sheet, was Diogo Costa, Joao Cancelo, Antonio Silva, Renato Veiga, Diogo Dalot, Vitinha, Joao Neves, Pedro Neto, Bruno Fernandes (captain), Francisco Trincao and Goncalo Ramos. The FAW report does not list which four players came in, and we have not seen the Denmark line-up to compare.

The bigger calls came after an hour. With the score 2-2, Jesus took off Trincao and Ramos on 59 minutes and sent on Carlos Forbs and Joao Felix. Forbs put Portugal ahead on 70 minutes, Sky Sports reports. Dalot and Vitinha then made way on 71 minutes, with Ruben Neves among the replacements.

How it worked out

Bruno Fernandes opened the scoring after 11 minutes when Joao Neves robbed Josh Sheehan, but David Brooks equalised almost at once. Sorba Thomas put Wales ahead five minutes before half-time, and Neto levelled three minutes later from Trincao's cross. After Forbs' goal, Neto was sent off for two yellow cards in two minutes, leaving Portugal to see out the final 16 minutes with ten men.

Portugal finish as Group A4 winners, and Sky says it was their first win since Cristiano Ronaldo retired from international football. Wales, who had already been consigned to the relegation play-offs, finish bottom.

Our view: Jesus needed a reaction after the Denmark defeat, and his substitutes delivered it. The win settles the group, but conceding twice to a side with nothing left to play for in the group, then losing Neto to a red card, will not end questions about Portugal's control of games.
