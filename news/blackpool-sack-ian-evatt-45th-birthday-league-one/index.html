Happy birthday, you're sacked: why Blackpool's owner ended Ian Evatt's year in charge hours after the club's tweet

By Decide Football · 2026-11-19

At 9am on Thursday, Blackpool's official account wished its head coach a happy birthday. "Have a great day, Gaffer!" the club posted on X. By the afternoon, Ian Evatt was out. "Blackpool sack head coach Ian Evatt on his birthday," talkSPORT posted.

The decision

Blackpool have sacked Evatt on his 45th birthday after a year in charge, following one win and six defeats in their past ten League One games, BBC Sport reports. His final match was Saturday's 1-0 defeat away to fellow strugglers AFC Wimbledon, which left the Tangerines immediately above the relegation zone. Evatt had said his squad was hit by a sickness bug on that trip.

BBC Radio Lancashire's Andy Bayes says owner-chairman Simon Sadler decided a change was needed with 33 league matches still to play. In a statement, the club thanked Evatt and said Steve Thompson will take charge for Saturday's game against Wigan Athletic.

The record

According to the BBC, Evatt won 23 of his 57 games in charge. He replaced Steve Bruce in October 2025, took 13 points from a possible 15 that December to lift Blackpool out of the bottom four, then won two of the next 15 before a strong finish secured survival. A run of one defeat in 13 from mid-March to the end of August raised hopes, but form has been poor since September.

In October, after a defeat by Mansfield, Evatt said he had "a huge amount of confidence" in his ability because of his record. That record includes the 2019-20 National League title with Barrow, promotion to League One with Bolton and the 2022-23 EFL Trophy.

What went wrong, according to the local view

Bayes points to a long injury list, especially at centre-back, where Steve Bruce signings Fraser Horsfall and Michael Ihiekwe have yet to play a league game this season. Supporters wanted more tactical flexibility; Evatt largely stuck with wing-backs, believing he lacked the players for anything else.

What comes next

Blackpool are now looking for their eighth permanent manager or head coach since Sadler bought the club in the summer of 2019, the BBC says. Thompson's first test is Wigan at Bloomfield Road on Saturday.
