Kompany is back for Bayern v Köln, and admits he made no decisions while his staff stayed unbeaten

By Decide Football · 2026-11-19

Vincent Kompany will be back on the Bayern Munich touchline for Friday's Bundesliga home game against Köln, a month after he collapsed at the training ground, Bundesliga.com reports. "I'm all fine now and am happy to be back with the team," he said at Thursday's pre-match press conference, his arm in a sling after a shoulder injury.

The decision he did not make

Kompany has been overseeing training since the start of the week. But for the month he was away, he says, he chose to stay out of the decision-making. "I didn't make any decisions," he said. "I was involved in discussions after the match, but more out of interest in how the decisions had been made. There's an important reason why I wasn't involved in the decision-making process. Making decisions is about having information. You get that when you're there on the ground."

He set out who did what. René Maric often does the tactical analysis, Aaron Danks plans training sessions, Floribert Ngalula is heavily involved in individual training, particularly for young players, and Daniel Fradley is involved in team meetings and analysis.

How the staff got on

With Danks and the other assistants in charge, Bayern beat Freiburg and Mainz in the Bundesliga either side of a draw with Borussia Dortmund, and also beat Atlético Madrid in the Champions League and Magdeburg in the DFB Cup. They remain unbeaten this season. We looked at one of those calls in how Danks set Bayern up against Atlético.

"I really enjoyed the Atlético match. It was one of the finest moments of my coaching career, even though I wasn't there," Kompany said.

On his collapse

"I was quite simply ill and perhaps didn't listen closely enough to my body," Kompany said. "It was certainly a shock. But it was a result of me carrying on when I really should've taken a break." He said every test had come back clear.

What to watch on Friday

Köln at the Allianz Arena opens Matchday 10. The question now is how quickly Kompany takes the decisions back from a staff that kept Bayern unbeaten without him. "I'd now like for everything to go back to normal again as quickly as possible," he said.
