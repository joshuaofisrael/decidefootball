Scaloni recalls Emiliano Martinez and plans four or five changes for Argentina's friendly against Costa Rica

By Decide Football · 2026-11-17

Lionel Scaloni has named his goalkeeper and promised a reshuffle for Argentina's last match of 2026. Speaking at Ezeiza on Monday, the Argentina coach said Emiliano Martinez will start Tuesday's international friendly against Costa Rica at the Estadio Mario Alberto Kempes in Cordoba, TN reports. Kick-off is 8pm local time (6pm ET).

The decision

"Emiliano will be in goal. In the previous game it was a risk and we trusted Gero," Scaloni said, referring to Geronimo Rulli, who started Friday's 2-1 defeat by Uruguay at La Bombonera. He said Argentina will make four or five changes, with the final training session deciding exactly which. He added that Argentina will not change their system.

Who could come in

TN expects Nicolas Paz to drop deeper alongside Enzo Fernandez and Alexis Mac Allister, which would open a place in attack for Gianluca Prestianni, with Matias Soule and Franco Mastantuono also options. Agustin Giay could replace Gonzalo Montiel at right-back, while Nicolas Tagliafico and Roman Vega compete at left-back. Chelsea defender Aaron Anselmino and Parma's Mariano Troilo are the only uncapped players in the squad and could make their debuts.

TN's predicted line-up still has Rulli in goal, written before Scaloni's comments, so the goalkeeper is the one position Scaloni has confirmed.

The bigger picture

Scaloni said Argentina are still rebuilding after the departure of Lionel Messi and others, and that the core group is now about 20 players rather than 12 or 14. He said players such as Cristian Romero and Leandro Paredes are hard to replace and that he will keep testing options.

Our view: recalling Martinez restores a fixed point after the Uruguay defeat, while changes elsewhere make this friendly a trial for players who want a place in that group of 20.
