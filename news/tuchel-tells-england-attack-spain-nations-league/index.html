Tuchel tells England to attack Spain and make it physical in Nations League decider

By Decide Football · 2026-11-14

Thomas Tuchel has made his choice for England's biggest test of the autumn: go after Spain. The England manager said his side want to attack Spain and make it a physical match in Sunday's Nations League fixture at the Metropolitano Stadium in Madrid, ESPN reports.

What is at stake

England beat Croatia 2-1 at Wembley on Thursday to keep their chance of topping the group alive. The Guardian explains that only a win over the world and European champions will do, because England's superior goal difference would then take them top, which in theory brings easier opposition in March's two-legged quarter-finals. Spain beat England 3-2 at Wembley in the opening game of the group, and the Guardian says they have lost only once in 43 matches, on penalties to Portugal in the 2025 Nations League final.

The tactical decision

Tuchel's main tweak this season, the Guardian reports, has been to give Jude Bellingham greater licence to drift from the No 10 role and overload either side, with others, including the full-backs, encouraged into unusual zones. The principles stay the same: aggression, physicality and intensity, including a high press. ESPN quotes Tuchel saying the reverse fixture's mindset must carry over and quotes him saying "no one is unbeatable."

Team news

Ezri Konsa and Djed Spence hope to return after minor injuries, Myles Lewis-Skelly is back from a one-game ban, and Alex Scott is out injured, according to the Guardian. Tuchel had only 11 players from the World Cup in his squad against Croatia.

Analysis: choosing to press and play front-foot football away to a side Tuchel said has forgotten how to lose, as reported by ESPN, is a calculated risk. It fits his stated aim of treating the match as a test of how far his team have come before Euro 2028.
