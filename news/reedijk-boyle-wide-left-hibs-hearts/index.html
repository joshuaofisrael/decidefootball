Reedijk's Wide-Left Call on Boyle Pays Off as Hibs Beat Hearts at Hampden

By Decide Football · 2026-11-05

Marink Reedijk caught Hearts off guard by playing Martin Boyle in an unfamiliar wide-left role in Sunday's Premier Sports Cup semi-final, and the Scottish Premiership side's forward responded with the goal that helped Hibs to a 2-1 win and a place in the final, the Edinburgh Evening News reported. It was Boyle's sixth goal in Edinburgh derbies.

Two calls on Boyle

The new head coach had already made a bold leadership decision, naming German right-back Felix Passlack captain ahead of Boyle, who had worn the armband consistently for two years while club captain Joe Newell was injured. Hibs had lost three and drawn one of Reedijk's opening matches before Hampden.

Boyle, who had missed the previous two games against Celtic and St Mirren with illness, said he was told on Saturday morning that he would start. "I didn't expect that," he said of the position. "It was kind of a free role, you can stay high and wide out there and can hit them where it hurts."

No grudge over the armband

Boyle said Reedijk had explained the captaincy change to him. "It didn't affect me one single bit," he said. "If that is the way forward he wants to go, then we will need to back the manager." Of the derby, he added: "Fair play to him on Sunday, he got it spot on."
