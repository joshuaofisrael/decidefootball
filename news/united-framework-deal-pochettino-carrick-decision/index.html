Manchester United reach a framework deal with Pochettino as Carrick's job is reviewed

By Decide Football · 2026-11-11

Manchester United have a framework agreement with Mauricio Pochettino for him to become head coach if Michael Carrick is sacked, The Guardian reported on 11 November. Carrick had not been dismissed, and Pochettino had not been appointed, in any club statement at the time of that report.

Jamie Jackson's article, updated at 8:52 a.m. Eastern time, said Carrick is expected to be sacked in line with a recommendation from director of football Jason Wilcox and chief executive Omar Berrada to Sir Jim Ratcliffe, who controls United's sporting policy. Wilcox and Berrada were meeting Ratcliffe in Monaco to put that recommendation, the report said. A United board meeting was scheduled for Wednesday, 11 November, at which one or more of the Glazer siblings, the majority owners, would be represented. There was "an expectation within the executive and in Carrick's camp that the sacking will be ratified there and that an announcement will follow," The Guardian wrote.

Pochettino is head coach of the United States men's national team and, the report said, signed a contract in August to 2030, but wants the United job after previous Premier League spells with Southampton, Tottenham and Chelsea. The Guardian said his contract includes a release clause of about $10 million (£7.5 million), with the compensation higher if he brings staff, including long-time assistant Jesús Pérez. He is due to take charge of the United States in Concacaf Nations League matches against Haiti on Saturday and Wednesday in Miami and Cincinnati.

Fabrizio Romano wrote on X on Wednesday morning that "Manchester United are advancing in talks to appoint Mauricio Pochettino as new head coach," that "Pochettino wants the job and talks are moving forward, as @TheAthleticFC reports," and that he is "the clear favorite waiting for club final decision." In a later post, Romano said United were "set for final internal meeting to proceed with the expected plan: Michael Carrick to be sacked," and that "Mauricio Pochettino’s $10m release clause is available and Man United are working on it + contract terms with his camp."

The Guardian also set out United's league position as 18th, in the relegation zone, with one Premier League win, against Ipswich in August, and three wins in all competitions, plus a Carabao Cup exit to Brighton in September. Those figures are as reported on 11 November.

This article records a reported framework deal and an expected decision, not a completed sacking or appointment. Only a club announcement, or a similarly clear confirmation after the Wednesday meeting, would settle Carrick's and Pochettino's positions.
