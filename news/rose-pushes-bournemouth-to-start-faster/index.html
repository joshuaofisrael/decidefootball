Marco Rose's fast-start plan works as Bournemouth win eighth in a row at Ipswich

By Decide Football · 2026-11-09

Marco Rose made fast starts a priority for Bournemouth, and his side delivered at Portman Road. Evanilson scored after five minutes and Bournemouth beat Ipswich Town 2-1 in the Premier League on Sunday, their eighth consecutive win in all competitions, the Guardian reported.

The decision in focus

Bournemouth are playing European football for the first time this season, and the Ipswich match came three days after a Europa League win at Sparta Prague. The Guardian says Rose has been "working hard" to make sure his team are ready to start Premier League games quickly after a Thursday fixture.

Rose explained his reasoning by pointing to the cost of slow starts. "We played Chelsea four weeks ago and we were two down after three minutes," he said. "That's why we learned and always good to score an early goal." In the previous weekend's 2-0 home win against Leeds, Bournemouth had a goal ruled out after 11 minutes.

"Last week I said we start slow and this week I said we start quite quick and score an early goal. I am a magician!"
Marco Rose, quoted by the Guardian, 8 November 2026

How the game went

Evanilson's opener came after Ipswich goalkeeper Christian Walton tried and failed to dribble past him. Bournemouth went 2-0 up 27 seconds into the second half, when Leif Davis deflected Ryan Christie's shot into his own net. Jack Clarke pulled one back from the penalty spot with 17 minutes left after Lewis Cook tripped Abdoul Ouattara, but Bournemouth held on.

Rose said he was pleased with the first 70 minutes but "much more pleased with the last 20 minutes. We suffered but we did it together."

The numbers

Eight straight wins equals the club record set under Eddie Howe in 2012-13, a season that ended in promotion from League One. Evanilson has now scored in five consecutive Premier League matches. The Premier League says no Bournemouth player had done that before. With seven goals this season, he has already beaten his total for the whole of last season.

How Decide Football reads it

Rose identified slow starts as the risk of the Thursday-Sunday schedule and built his preparation around fixing them. At Ipswich it worked twice, with early goals in both halves. The test is whether Bournemouth can keep it up through a run of European and league fixtures.
