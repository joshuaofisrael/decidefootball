Pocognoli recalls David Bates to the Scotland squad after a seven-year gap

By Decide Football · 2026-11-12

Scotland head coach Sébastien Pocognoli has called up centre-back David Bates for this month's UEFA Nations League matches, after Luis Binks withdrew injured. The Scottish FA confirmed the change on Wednesday.

Bates, 30, has not played for Scotland since 2019. All four of his caps came while he was at Hamburg, the Scottish FA says, and the BBC adds that they were all won under Alex McLeish.

Why Bates

The Scottish FA says Bates has been an ever-present in defence this season for Amedspor, who are third in the Turkish Süper Lig. BBC Sport reports he has started all 11 of their matches. Since leaving Hamburg he has also played in Belgium for Cercle Brugge, Mechelen and Standard Liège, and spent the 2021/22 season at Aberdeen.

He joins Luke Graham, Jack Hendry and Ryan Porteous as the central defenders in the squad, and left-back Kieran Tierney can also play in Pocognoli's preferred back three, according to the BBC. Binks, of Brøndby, had won his first three caps in Scotland's opening Nations League fixtures.

The fixtures

Scotland host North Macedonia at Hampden on Friday and travel to Switzerland on Monday, BBC Sport reports. North Macedonia have no points in the group, while Switzerland have won every game so far.
