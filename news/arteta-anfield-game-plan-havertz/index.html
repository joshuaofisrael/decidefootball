How Arteta's game plan stopped Liverpool from landing a shot on target at Anfield

By Decide Football · 2026-11-02

Date checked: 21 November 2026. The score, the table gap and the shot count below are taken from Arsenal’s match report of 1 November 2026 and Sky Sports’ report of 2 November 2026. Both pages still carried these details when checked.

Mikel Arteta’s Arsenal won 1-0 at Liverpool on Sunday 1 November 2026. Arsenal’s report calls it the club’s first win at Anfield in any competition since September 2012, the end of a run of 16 trips without a win there, and the first clean sheet there in the Premier League since that 2012 victory.

The goal, then the spell without the ball

Sky Sports times the goal at the 16th minute. Martin Odegaard cut out Alisson’s clearance, the ball went to Declan Rice on the right, and Kai Havertz finished the low cross at the far post. Arsenal’s report says that goal made Havertz the top-scoring German in Premier League history, on 46, passing Ilkay Gundogan’s 45. Sky reports the same 46.

Sky counted 17 Liverpool efforts, none of which forced David Raya into a save, and called it the first time on record that Liverpool had failed to land a shot on target at home. Arsenal’s facts section is more precise about the record: the first time since 2003/04, in Liverpool’s 442nd league match at Anfield in that span. The same Arsenal report also describes Raya getting a hand to a Virgil van Dijk header. The two accounts agree on the clean sheet and on a shot-on-target record. They do not describe every attempt in the same sentence, and this page will not invent a reconciliation.

Sky also says officials missed a tug by Jurrien Timber on Alexander Isak in the first half, which that report says might have led to a penalty. That is Sky’s description of the incident.

Where the win left the table

Arsenal’s report says the result put them back on top, with 24 points from nine games, the most by any side after nine games of a Premier League season since Arsenal themselves in 2022/23, who also had 24. Sky says that was three points clear of Manchester City and nine clear of Liverpool. The club’s next game, the report said, was away to Slavia Prague in the Champions League on the Wednesday.

What the spell was

The part that belongs with tactics is the long spell after the goal, spent keeping Liverpool away from Raya. That is the defensive height the low block entry describes, even though neither match report uses the phrase. Arteta’s later complaint was the other problem: he told Arsenal to be more chaotic against low blocks after the win over Hull. Anfield was an afternoon when his own side were the ones happy to stay compact. More from this league is on the Premier League page.
