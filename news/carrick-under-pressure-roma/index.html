Carrick went into Roma nine games winless. United later confirmed he had left

By Decide Football · 2026-11-02

This page is the picture of Michael Carrick’s job on the eve of Manchester United’s Champions League match with Roma, not the state of the job now. The club has since confirmed that he left, and that Mauricio Pochettino replaced him. Those later announcements are linked below. Date checked: 21 November 2026.

What the previews said

Radio Times, in a page published on 3 November 2026 and still carrying this account when checked, said Carrick was under immense pressure before Roma’s visit to Old Trafford. United had lost 2-0 at Chelsea on the Saturday, with Lisandro Martinez sent off. The winless run in all competitions stood at nine games. Radio Times said that had not happened to United since 1988. It also said Carrick had been given the club’s backing the previous week, and that another defeat at Old Trafford could cost him the job.

The same page had United 17th in the Premier League, above the relegation zone on goal difference, and 21st in the Champions League table after defeats against Atletico Madrid and Como. It did not print a score for the Como match, so this page does not either. Roma, under Gian Piero Gasperini, were unbeaten in Serie A, with seven wins and three draws, and had a league-high 28 goals.

Sports Illustrated’s preview, also dated 3 November 2026, said Martinez’s ban covered domestic games, so he was available against Roma, and that his form meant he should be left out. That is the preview’s lineup conclusion. It is not a club team sheet.

What happened next

United did not lose the game the previews were worrying about. Our match report, citing BBC Sport and Sky Sports, records a 3-2 win, with Shea Lacey coming on at 2-1 down and setting up Matheus Cunha’s winner. The nine-game run ended that night. The job did not last the month.

On 12 November the club confirmed Carrick had left, which we reported from the club’s own post in Manchester United confirm Michael Carrick has left. A day later the club confirmed Pochettino, reported in Manchester United confirm Mauricio Pochettino as head coach. The framework talks that sat between the Roma win and those announcements are in the piece on the board’s review.
