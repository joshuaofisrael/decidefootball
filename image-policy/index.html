Image policy

Most images on Decide Football are AI-generated illustrations. They do not show real events, real photographs or real people as they actually appeared, and they are not photos taken at any match. These images carry an "AI-generated illustration" label linking to this page.

Some articles use Creative Commons photographs. These are credited under the image with the photographer, the licence and a link to the original.
