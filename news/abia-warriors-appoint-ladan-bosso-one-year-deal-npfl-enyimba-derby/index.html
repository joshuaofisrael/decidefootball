Winless in seven and 19th, Abia Warriors bring back Ladan Bosso, and his first game is the Enyimba derby

By Decide Football · 2026-11-20

Abia Warriors have gone back to someone they know. "Abia Warriors have announced the appointment of Ladan Bosso as their new head coach," NaijaNowFb posted on X on Thursday, adding that he takes over after the resignation of the previous coach. The post spells that coach's name "Imama Akpabo"; Nigerian reports name him as Imama Amapakabo.

The decision

Foot Africa says Bosso has signed a one-year contract with a clear brief: keep the Umuahia club in the Nigeria Premier Football League. Abia are 19th after 13 matches, close to the relegation places. Daily Post says they have not won in their last seven league games, and that Amapakabo resigned earlier this week.

Bosso is a familiar name. Daily Post says he coached Abia in their early NPFL years and returns 11 years after his first spell. He has also coached Nigeria's Flying Eagles, the under-20 side. The X post adds that he was most recently technical director at Barau FC.

What he said

"It's indeed a herculean task, but with the determination, commitment and professionalism of all the variables involved, technically and administratively, it's going to be an achievable task," Bosso said, according to Foot Africa.

Why this choice

In a relegation fight, clubs often choose experience over a long project, and a one-year deal fits that. Bosso knows the club and the league, so he needs less time to settle. He has very little time anyway.

What comes next

His first match is Sunday's Abia derby against Enyimba, Daily Post reports. A home crowd, a local rival and a seven-game winless run make it a tough first test. More from Nigeria is on our NPFL page, and other coaches fighting for their jobs are in Under Pressure.
