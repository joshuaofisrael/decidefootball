One win all season: TS Galaxy turn to Bosnia again and hand their rescue job to Admir Adzem

By Decide Football · 2026-11-20

TS Galaxy have made their call on how to save their season. "The dawn of a new era is here! We are absolutely delighted to announce Admir Adzem as the new Head Coach of TS Galaxy FC," the club posted on X on Thursday. The same post welcomes "Coach Ademir", a slip in the spelling of his name.

The decision

Adzem replaces Bernard Parker, who left after a poor start. Caretaker Esau Mtsweni had been in charge since. According to KickOff, the Bosnian arrives with his own assistant, Kenan Handzic.

The numbers explain the change. KickOff says Galaxy have one win from 12 Betway Premiership matches and sit 12th. SuperSport also has them 12th with one league win, though it counts nine league games, and notes they were knocked out of the Carling Knockout Cup by Kaizer Chiefs.

A familiar route

This is not a new idea for Galaxy. SuperSport points out that Adzem follows two other Bosnian coaches into the job, Sead Ramović and Adnan Beganović. A former full-back with 14 caps for Bosnia and Herzegovina, Adzem coached his home club FK Željezničar and has also worked in the United Arab Emirates.

Choosing a coach who has not managed in South Africa is a risk in a relegation fight, where knowing the league helps. Galaxy appear to be betting that the approach which worked with earlier Bosnian coaches can work again, and bringing in his own assistant suggests Adzem will be allowed to set up his staff his way.

What comes next

Galaxy return to league action against Stellenbosch on 27 November, KickOff says, which gives Adzem about a week of training before his first match. Follow more decisions like this on our South African Premiership page.
