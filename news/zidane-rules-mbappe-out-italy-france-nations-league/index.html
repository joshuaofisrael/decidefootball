Zidane takes 'zero risk' and rules Mbappé out of France's Nations League match in Italy

By Decide Football · 2026-11-12

France head coach Zinedine Zidane has ruled Kylian Mbappé out of Thursday's UEFA Nations League match against Italy in Milan, choosing caution over a hamstring problem. Zidane announced the decision at his pre-match press conference on Wednesday evening, TF1 Info reports.

Zidane said the captain had felt a small pain and that France would take no risk with him, according to 20 Minutes and AFP. He added that he hoped the problem was minor and that Mbappé could be available for Sunday's match against Turkey in Bordeaux.

The decision in context

Mbappé trained normally on Monday at the start of the camp but did not take part in Wednesday morning's session at Clairefontaine, 20 Minutes reports. It will be his fourth missed France match in a row, TF1 Info notes: he scored the winner in Zidane's first game in charge, against Turkey on 25 September, before going off with a knee problem and missing the next three.

To cover the absence, Zidane called up Paris Saint-Germain's Senny Mayulu, who was already at Clairefontaine with the under-21 squad, per 20 Minutes. The same report lists a long list of other absentees, including Aurélien Tchouaméni, Marcus Thuram, Pierre Kalulu, Ousmane Dembélé, William Saliba and Jules Koundé.

What is at stake

France lead their Nations League group after three wins and a draw from four games, 20 Minutes says, so Zidane can afford to protect his captain for the final group match against Turkey.
