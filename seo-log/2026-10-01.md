# 2026-10-01 — Toss-up mean-delta tool

**Date:** 1 October 2026  
**Site:** decidefootball.com (Joshua Israel Ventures LLC)  
**Action:** Add one indexable editorial URL with a client-side calculator. No fixture indexing. No paid host. No projection API.

## Data reviewed

- Live site is HTTPS 200 on GitHub Pages. Eight editorial URLs were already indexable: `/about/`, `/methodology/`, `/guide/`, `/guide/start-sit/`, `/guide/waiver-radar/`, `/guide/listed-status/`, `/guide/rankings/`, and `/guide/add-drop/` (PR #14).
- Homepage and fixture sports URLs stay `noindex` / `Disallow` until licensed GREEN data. That includes `/start-sit/` and `/add-drop/`.
- No Search Console query data yet, so this choice is from the cluster and the code, not from a query report.
- `src/lib/recommendations.ts` already defines the desk line as `TOSS_UP_DELTA = 1.5` and uses it in `recommendStartSit` / `recommendAddDrop`. The cards print that gap to one decimal via `round1`.

## Why this action

A thin reading-guide leaf about certainty would repeat `/guide/start-sit/`, which already teaches the thin / lean / clear / strong bands. A methodology FAQ-only pass would restate a line that page already prints, and it would not open calculator or comparison intent.

Competitors serve that intent with their own projection APIs. This desk can serve it without licensed player rows: the visitor types the means, and the page applies the line the cards already use. The URL is distinct from `/guide/start-sit/` (how to read the lineup card) and `/guide/add-drop/` (how to read roster churn).

## What shipped

- `/guide/toss-up/` with `index,follow`, Organization, WebPage, FAQPage, and breadcrumb JSON-LD (Home → Guides → Toss-up tool).
- A client-side tool: optional labels (placeholders Player A / Player B), required means, optional floor and ceiling. The lean is `meanDeltaLean`, which `recommendStartSit` now calls, so there is one 1.5-point threshold. Absolute mean delta is the rounded gap. The sentence switches between start/sit and add/drop. The math does not.
- Floor and ceiling are display and weighing copy only. Under 1.5 the page says the pair is a toss-up on mean alone, that listed status still outranks numbers, and that the result is not gambling advice and not an official projection.
- Fixture-mode robots Allow and the sitemap gain `/guide/toss-up/` only. Disallow `/start-sit/` and `/add-drop/` are unchanged. Homepage stays `noindex` in fixture mode.
- The guides hub lists five reading notes and the toss-up tool. Footer, About, Methodology, the start/sit guide, the add/drop guide, and the rankings guide link the tool. `public/llms.txt` lists the URL. Sample `/start-sit/` and `/add-drop/` indexes link the tool and stay `noindex`.

## Why not the alternatives

- **Certainty leaf.** `/guide/start-sit/` already owns that grade. A second page would cannibalize it and would not add a tool.
- **Methodology FAQ only.** The 1.5-point line is already on the methodology page. Another FAQ would not open calculator intent and would not give the visitor a place to apply the rule.
- **Indexing fixture desks.** Player, start/sit, add/drop, and the rest of the sports URLs still use sample data. They stay out of Allow and out of the sitemap until licensed GREEN sports data replaces fixtures. This change does not call BALLDONTLIE and does not buy a data feed.

## What did not change

- No player, start/sit, injury, is-playing, waiver-wire, ranking, add/drop, week, slate, or watchlist URL was added to Allow or the sitemap.
- Ads stay gated. Fonts stay self-hosted. No analytics IDs were added. LLC ownership stays footer-only. No contact email or address was invented.
- No backtest, official-accuracy, or NFL-affiliation claim. No gambling or sportsbook framing.
