Iraola faces questions on Trey Nyoni before Liverpool's Fenerbahce test

By Decide Football · 2026-11-03

Liverpool head coach Andoni Iraola held his pre-match press conference in Istanbul on Tuesday ahead of the club's Champions League Matchday 4 tie against Fenerbahce. Liverpool summarised the topics on X:

“Andoni Iraola press conference: Fenerbahce challenge, Istanbul, Trey Nyoni and more 🎙️”
— Liverpool FC (@LFC), 3 November 2026

The mention of young midfielder Trey Nyoni puts the spotlight on how Iraola uses his squad in Europe. The full press conference is linked from the club's post.

Iraola's selection for Istanbul follows Sunday's 1-0 home defeat by Arsenal, in which Liverpool had 17 attempts but none on target. It was the first time on record that Liverpool failed to register a shot on target in a Premier League home game, and it left them nine points behind the leaders.

Cody Gakpo also spoke to the media before the tie.
