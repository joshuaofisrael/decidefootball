Sassuolo appoint Roberto D'Aversa after sacking Alberto Aquilani

By Decide Football · 2026-11-11

Sassuolo have appointed Roberto D'Aversa as men's first-team manager in Serie A, a day after the club sacked Alberto Aquilani.

In an official statement dated 9 November, U.S. Sassuolo Calcio said it had relieved Aquilani and his staff of their duties. The club thanked the coach and his collaborators for their commitment and professionalism and wished them well.

A day later, the club announced in English that D'Aversa is the new manager. His staff was listed as Salvatore Sullo, Marco Piccione, Marco Riggio, Paolo Rongoni, Francesco Petrungaro, Andrea Rinaldi, Simone Vignudelli and Marco Bizzarri. The club said D'Aversa's first training session would take place on the afternoon of 10 November, and that his first press conference would be at 12:30 on Thursday, 12 November, at the Mapei Football Center.

Journalist Fabrizio Romano had already reported the two moves on X on 9 November, writing that Sassuolo had sacked Aquilani with immediate effect and that D'Aversa had "agreed to become new Sassuolo head coach until the end of the season." The club's own English statement does not specify the length of D'Aversa's contract. The official notices are the confirmation that Aquilani has left and that D'Aversa is in the job.

This is a club decision on the Serie A bench, not a change of formation or selection by the outgoing manager. D'Aversa's first public remarks are due at Thursday's press conference. Until then, the club has not, in the statements fetched here, set out a playing brief beyond naming the new manager and his staff.
