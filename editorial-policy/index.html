Editorial policy

What we cover

We report on decisions by managers and head coaches: who they pick, how they set up, when they change things, and the squad, transfer and leadership calls they make. Every article names the competition and centres on one manager's decision.

Sourcing and accuracy

Each article is built from named, linkable sources: official club and competition channels, press conferences, and established news organisations. We link to every source we use at the foot of the article. We quote exactly and never invent quotes, scores, statistics or facts. Where reports conflict or a claim is unconfirmed, we say so or leave it out.

Articles are researched and drafted with the help of AI tools and then checked line by line against the linked sources before publication.

Independence

We are not affiliated with any club, league, governing body or betting company. No one pays for coverage.

Corrections

If we get something wrong we correct it promptly and, for any significant change, add a note to the article saying what changed. Report an error to hello@decidefootball.com with the article link.

Images

Images are either AI-generated illustrations, labelled as such and explained in our image policy, or Creative Commons photographs credited with the photographer, licence and source.

Style

We write in UK English and say football.
