About

Decide Football is a news site about one thing: the decisions made by managers and head coaches in British football. We cover team selection, tactical and formation changes, substitutions, rotation, dropping and recalling players, captaincy, penalty-taker choices, and the squad and transfer calls managers make and explain – plus the decisions behind sackings and the managers under pressure.

Every story centres on a specific manager's decision, the reasoning given for it and what followed. We quote managers, clubs and journalists exactly, link to the original post or report, and never invent quotes, scores or facts.
