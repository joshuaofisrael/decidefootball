Lens appoint Didier Digard as head coach until 2028 after sacking Cahuzac

By Decide Football · 2026-11-14

Lens have chosen Didier Digard to steady a season that has fallen apart. The Ligue 1 club announced on X on Friday evening that Digard will be head coach until June 2028, a day after relieving Yannick Cahuzac of his duties.

Why Lens made the change

Results forced the decision. Reuters reports that Cahuzac won three of his nine games in all competitions and lost the other six, a run that included a 9-1 defeat by Como in the Champions League, sandwiched between league losses to Lille and Marseille. Cahuzac had stepped up from assistant after Dino Toppmoeller was dismissed in September, making him the second coach to go this season. Lens sit 11th in Ligue 1 with 13 points, according to Reuters, a sharp drop for a side that won the French Cup and finished second last season.

Why Digard

In its official statement, the club said every conversation with candidates was led by its decision-making structure under president Joseph Oughourlian, and that a strong consensus formed around the 40-year-old. Lens point to his spell at Nice, where he took over mid-season and went 10 wins, 7 draws and 4 defeats, an average of 1.76 points a game, while reaching a Conference League quarter-final. The club also cites two consecutive Ligue 1 survivals without the play-offs on the division's smallest budget. Le Havre named Valérien Ismaël as his replacement on Thursday.

The statement says Digard will take his first session on Saturday and will run an audit of the staff, with changes to follow if needed.

What comes next

Lens host Toulouse in Ligue 1 after the international break, Reuters says. Analysis: Digard's record is built on organising sides with limited resources; at Lens he inherits a squad that finished second last season, so the test is whether he can restore that level quickly enough to rescue a European place.
