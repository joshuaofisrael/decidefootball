De la Fuente leaves Lamine Yamal at home for Spain's match in Czechia and denies special treatment

By Decide Football · 2026-11-12

Spain head coach Luis de la Fuente has defended his decision to leave Lamine Yamal out of Thursday's UEFA Nations League match against Czechia in Prague. Speaking at his pre-match press conference, De la Fuente said the winger has inflammation in his knee and that he preferred him to stay at home and train with Barcelona rather than make the trip, Marca reports.

De la Fuente insisted the 19-year-old was getting "no privilege", adding that Spain would have done the same for any other player, according to Marca.

How the decision came about

Lamine Yamal finished Barcelona's La Liga match against Atlético Madrid on Sunday with discomfort in his right knee, and after tests it was decided he would stay in Barcelona to continue his recovery, RTVE reported on Monday. The Spanish federation said he and Mikel Oyarzabal, who was recovering from flu, were expected to join the squad after the Czechia game and be available against England on Sunday 15 November. De la Fuente called up Liverpool winger Víctor Muñoz to cover.

Marca says the inflammation is not serious and that Lamine Yamal is following a recovery plan set by the Barcelona and Spain medical staff, with the aim of joining up on Friday. It adds that he has started all 20 of his games for club and country this season, more than 1,600 minutes in total.

What it means

The call keeps Spain's most-used attacker fresh for Sunday's game against England at the Metropolitano, and for Barcelona's next La Liga fixture against Villarreal, per Marca. Whether he starts on Sunday will depend on how the knee responds this week.
