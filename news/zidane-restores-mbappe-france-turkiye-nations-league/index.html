Zidane restores Mbappé for France's final Nations League group game against Türkiye

By Decide Football · 2026-11-15

Zinedine Zidane has brought his captain back. Kylian Mbappé is available for France's Nations League match against Türkiye in Bordeaux tonight, the France head coach confirmed on Saturday, Managing Madrid reports, after the Real Madrid forward sat out Thursday's 3-1 defeat to Italy with a minor muscular problem.

The call on Mbappé

Mbappé had wanted to play in Milan, but Zidane chose not to risk him. On Saturday, Zidane would not say whether he would use Mbappé through the middle or on the wing. He also dismissed reports of a strained relationship with his captain as nonsense and said their relationship was going perfectly well.

Other changes

The Italy game was Zidane's first defeat as France coach, and one change is forced. Dayot Upamecano has withdrawn from the squad for personal reasons, with Ibrahima Konaté expected to replace him, Get French Football News reports. It adds that Wolfsburg right-back Sael Kumbedi could start and that L'Équipe's predicted side has Mbappé leading the line between Bradley Barcola and Michael Olise. Türkiye have already been relegated to League B.

Our view: tonight is a chance to see how Zidane fixes the defence that Italy exposed and how he fits Mbappé into his attack. Both answers will shape his plans for March.
