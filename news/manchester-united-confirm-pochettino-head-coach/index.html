Manchester United confirm Mauricio Pochettino as head coach after Carrick exit

By Decide Football · 2026-11-13

Manchester United have chosen Mauricio Pochettino as the man to lift them out of the Premier League relegation zone. The club said on X on Friday morning that "Mauricio Pochettino has agreed to become the Manchester United men's head coach", a day after Michael Carrick left the role.

The appointment completes the framework deal we reported on Wednesday.

Two more games with the USMNT first

US Soccer confirmed that Pochettino will leave his role once the current camp ends, but that he and his staff will coach the national team in both Concacaf Nations League quarter-final legs against Haiti, on 14 and 17 November. He was appointed in September 2024 and leaves with a record of 21 wins, 12 defeats and two draws from 35 matches, including a run to the 2025 Gold Cup final and the last 16 of the 2026 World Cup on home soil.

The terms

Sky Sports reports that Pochettino will sign a two-and-a-half-year contract, to the summer of 2029, and that United will pay US Soccer £7.4m in compensation in line with his buyout clause. According to Sky, United told US Soccer they were happy for him to take both Haiti games out of respect, and plans are being made to fly him straight from Cincinnati to Manchester.

Sky says he will meet the players when the squad returns to training on Thursday, leaving three training days before his first match in charge, away at Liverpool. He will be United's eighth permanent manager since Sir Alex Ferguson retired in 2013.

What the decision means

United's board has gone for an experienced Premier League head coach rather than another interim appointment, and has accepted a short wait to get him. The cost is preparation time: Pochettino will have less than a week with his new squad before an away game at Anfield, with the club having won once in its first ten league matches.
