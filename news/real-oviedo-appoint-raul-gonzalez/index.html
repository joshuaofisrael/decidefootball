Real Oviedo appoint Raúl González as head coach until June 2028

By Decide Football · 2026-11-11

Real Oviedo has appointed Raúl González Blanco as head coach of the first team until June 2028, the club said in an official statement. "The blue entity has reached an agreement to hire the Madrid native as the new head coach of the first team until June 2028," the club wrote.

The statement gives Raúl's date of birth as 27 June 1977 and notes that he spent most of his playing career at Real Madrid, later turning out for Schalke 04, Al-Sadd and New York Cosmos before retiring in 2015. As a coach, Oviedo said he joined Real Madrid's youth system in 2018, later led Juvenil A to the UEFA Youth League title, and then spent six seasons in charge of Real Madrid Castilla.

The club said he will take charge for the first time this Saturday, against Girona at the Carlos Tartiere. It also said the new coach "will not make any statements until his official presentation."

The appointment, contract end date and first-match details are as published on Real Oviedo's official website. The club's English-language statement does not name his predecessor or give a reason for the vacancy.
