Maresca says injury forced Guéhi and Gvardiol half-time changes in Premier League loss at Forest

By Decide Football · 2026-11-08

Enzo Maresca replaced both Marc Guéhi and Joško Gvardiol at half-time of Manchester City's 3-1 Premier League defeat at Nottingham Forest on Saturday, and said afterwards that the double change was down to injury, NBC Sports reported.

“Marc Guehi asked for the change because of injury, Josko [Gvardiol] during the week had some small issues.”

The selection before kick-off

Maresca made three changes for the trip, with Rúben Dias, Gvardiol and Jérémy Doku coming into the starting XI and Matheus Nunes, Nico O'Reilly and Phil Foden dropping to the bench, according to City's team news. The back line in front of Gianluigi Donnarumma was Abdukodir Khusanov, Dias, Guéhi and Gvardiol. Losing two of those four at the interval meant reshaping the defence for the second half.

Injury, not a hook

Mistakes and slow play from both defenders had looked like the reason for the change, NBC Sports noted, but Maresca's explanation points to fitness rather than a tactical rebuke. He was also blunt about the first half as a whole, saying his side lost the duels and second balls and were 2-1 down by the time they tried to react.

What the decision leaves behind

The defeat was City's third straight in the Premier League and extended their run without a clean sheet to eight games, with former City prospect Liam Delap scoring twice for Forest. If Guéhi's problem lingers, NBC Sports pointed out, the defensive options behind him are thin, which makes the timing of the international break important for City. Maresca shrugged off the slump, pointing out that City had won their first seven games before losing three in a row.
