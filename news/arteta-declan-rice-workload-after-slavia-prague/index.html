Arteta defends Declan Rice’s full 90 after Slavia Prague

By Decide Football · 2026-11-07

Mikel Arteta has defended his decision to keep Declan Rice on for the full 90 minutes in Arsenal’s Champions League win away to Slavia Prague, saying the midfielder’s workload fitted the club’s recovery plan.

The decision

Asked after the 2-1 victory whether playing Rice for the entire match had been the plan, Arteta told Arsenal’s own transcript of the press conference: “The game required it, and he was also rested against Fleetwood. He had a week to recover before Liverpool, it’s his second game in a week and we expect our players to be able to do that.”

That answer is the managerial decision at the centre of this piece: Arteta chose minutes for Rice in Prague rather than protecting him with an earlier substitution, and he publicly justified the call with prior rest and recovery time.

League and competition context

The fixture sits in the UEFA Champions League, but Arteta’s comments also speak to Premier League selection pressure. The Guardian’s 6 November Premier League news roundup said Arteta insisted Rice is not being asked to play too many games and that Arsenal are managing his training load and minutes more carefully than in any previous season. Those are Arteta’s assurances as reported; they do not by themselves prove a lower minute total than last season.

Arsenal.com’s full presser transcript also covers Arteta’s criticism of a second-half drop-off, praise for Viktor Gyökeres and concern about the chances Slavia created. The Rice question is the selection call that matters for Decide Football’s manager-decision brief.

Why the call matters

Rice is central to how Arteta structures both Premier League and Champions League midfields. Playing him for 90 minutes on the road after a cup rest is a statement that the head coach trusts the recovery window more than the public fatigue narrative. The counter-risk is accumulation across a congested November. Arteta’s public answer puts the responsibility on planning: Fleetwood rest, a week before Liverpool, then a second start in seven days that he expects senior players to handle.

Until Arsenal’s next Premier League XI is named, the Prague full 90 remains the clearest recent evidence of how Arteta is balancing Rice’s Champions League and league demands.
