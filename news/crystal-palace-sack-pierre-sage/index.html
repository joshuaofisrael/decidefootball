Crystal Palace part company with Pierre Sage after five months, with the club bottom of the Premier League

By Decide Football · 2026-11-12

Crystal Palace have dismissed manager Pierre Sage after five months in charge, with the club bottom of the Premier League. Palace said in a statement, reported by BBC Sport, that Sage "has left his role as manager" and that three members of his coaching staff, Jamal Alioui, Damien Della Santa and Sandy Guichard, have also left.

The club said an update on appointing a new manager would follow in due course.

Why the board acted now

Sunday's 3-1 defeat by Liverpool was Palace's fourth Premier League loss in a row, and Sage's side won only two of their ten league games, according to BBC Sport. The BBC adds that Palace have conceded 24 league goals, the worst defensive record in the division, and sit 21st in the Europa League table with one win from four.

ESPN reports that Sage, 47, had signed a three-year contract in June after leaving Lens, where he finished second in Ligue 1 and won the Coupe de France. It lists a 5-0 defeat by Brighton on 18 October, a 2-0 home loss to Newcastle and a 2-0 defeat at Tottenham among the results that put him under pressure. He succeeded Oliver Glasner, who left after winning the FA Cup and the Conference League.

The likely replacement

Palace have not named a successor. BBC Sport reports that Thomas Frank, formerly head coach of Brentford and Tottenham, is in advanced talks to take over. The international break gives the new manager time with the squad before the next league fixture.
