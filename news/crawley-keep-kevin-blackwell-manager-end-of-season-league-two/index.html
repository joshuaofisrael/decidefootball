Bottom of the EFL and six points adrift, Crawley have bet on a 67-year-old caretaker. Here is why

By Decide Football · 2026-11-19

Crawley Town have stopped looking. "Crawley Town Football Club is delighted to announce that Kevin Blackwell will be staying on as Manager until the end of the 2026/27 season," the club posted on X on Thursday.

The decision

Blackwell, 67, took interim charge after Colin Kazim-Richards was sacked on 21 October with Crawley bottom of League Two and six points adrift, BBC Sport reports. They are unbeaten in the two league games he has overseen, a 1-1 draw with Tranmere Rovers and a 1-0 win at Oldham. They are still bottom and six points behind 22nd-placed Rochdale, though with three games in hand on them.

Why Crawley chose him

The club said Blackwell "made a strong impression on club leadership, making him a strong candidate for the role as the weeks progressed". Its statement added: "Kevin's leadership skills, work ethic and desire have made a strong impression on club staff and the first-team squad, and the club's leadership team are committed to ensuring the positive mentality remains in the building as we approach a run of crucial games."

It is a short-term deal by design. The appointment runs to the end of the season, which leaves the club free to look again in the summer.

The man in charge

Blackwell has managed Leeds United, Luton Town and Sheffield United, but this is his first permanent managerial job since leaving Bury in 2013, according to the BBC. He is Crawley's sixth permanent managerial appointment in four years. The club says he will make further coaching appointments in the coming weeks, following the recent arrival of Jordan Tait.

The decision came weeks after Crawley were knocked out of the FA Cup first round 2-0 by seventh-tier Wimborne Town.

What comes next

Crawley host Fleetwood Town in League Two on Saturday at 15:00 GMT, Blackwell's first game since being given the job permanently.
