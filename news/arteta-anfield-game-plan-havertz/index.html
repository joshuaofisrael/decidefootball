How Arteta's game plan stopped Liverpool from landing a shot on target at Anfield

By Decide Football · 2026-11-02

Mikel Arteta's Arsenal won 1-0 at Liverpool on Sunday 1 November, their first win at Anfield since 2012 and the end of a run of 16 visits without a victory there.

The goal came in the 16th minute. Martin Odegaard cut out a clearance from Alisson, the ball was moved on to Declan Rice on the right, and his low cross was tapped in at the far post by Kai Havertz.

After that, Arsenal's approach was about protecting the lead. Liverpool had 17 attempts but none forced David Raya into a save. According to Arsenal, it was the first time on record that Liverpool failed to land a shot on target in a Premier League game at Anfield. Jurrien Timber made important blocks. Sky Sports noted that officials missed a tug by Timber on Alexander Isak in the first half which might have led to a penalty.

The result took Arsenal top of the Premier League with 24 points from nine games, three points clear of Manchester City and nine clear of Liverpool. Havertz's goal also made him the top-scoring German in Premier League history, with 46. Arsenal's next game is away to Slavia Prague in the Champions League on Wednesday.
