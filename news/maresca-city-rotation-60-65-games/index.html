Maresca says City cannot use the same players across 60 to 65 games

By Decide Football · 2026-11-06

Enzo Maresca has publicly defended rotation at Manchester City, saying a season of 60 to 65 matches makes it impossible to field the same players every time.

The club posted his comments on X on 6 November 2026:

“The important thing is that, day after day, I know the players better. This is the best thing. It depends on the game, we have 60-65 games so it’s impossible to always use the same players. I’m quite lucky I have many good ones to choose from, depending on what we need for different games.”
Manchester City (@ManCity), 6 November 2026

The decision in focus

Maresca's point is a manager's selection problem, not a vague fitness slogan. He ties team picking to match demands and to a still growing feel for a large City squad. Knowing the players better day after day is, in his words, the best thing about the job right now.

That framing matters for Premier League weekends and midweek cups alike. When City rotate, Maresca wants the choice read as squad management across a heavy calendar, not as a vote of no confidence in whoever sits. He also stresses the luxury of options: many good players, chosen for what each game needs.

How Decide Football reads it

Managers under scrutiny for changed XIs often reach for fixture congestion. Maresca went further by putting a concrete 60 to 65 game band on the season and by owning the learning curve with his group. Until results turn against him, that is the public logic for why familiar names may sit and others start.
