Le Bris's Sunderland overhaul pays off as Talbi starts and Black Cats beat Chelsea

By Decide Football · 2026-11-09

Régis Le Bris made sweeping changes to his Sunderland side for Sunday's Premier League match against Chelsea, and they paid off with a 1-0 win that lifted the Black Cats off the bottom of the table.

The decision in focus

Sunderland had lost at home to Dinamo Zagreb on Thursday, a defeat the Guardian described as the "latest alarming performance" and one that "felt like a season watershed". Le Bris responded with what the Guardian called a "cull": only the captain, Granit Xhaka, Thomas Meunier and goalkeeper Robin Roefs kept their places.

Chemsdine Talbi was among those brought in, making his first Premier League start of the season. He had two "zesty incisions" in the opening quarter of an hour, according to the Guardian. The first led to a blocked shot from Trai Hume, and the second set up a clear chance for Brian Brobbey. Talbi also fired a shot just wide on the break late on.

How the match went

The rotation was tested within three minutes when Xhaka went off injured. Sunderland were also without Dan Ballard, who was injured, and Omar Alderete, who was suspended. The patched-up defence still kept Chelsea quiet. Enzo Le Fée scored the only goal from the penalty spot just after the half-hour, after Levi Colwill brought down Brobbey.

Le Bris pointed to Brobbey's role after the match. "Against a team that wants to press like that it's so important to have a target man," he said. He described the performance as "the identity, the intensity, the togetherness, the way we managed the game".

Why it matters

The Premier League's review notes that it was Sunderland's second league win of the season, with both coming at the Stadium of Light. It was also their third consecutive win over Chelsea. Their next four home league games are against Tottenham Hotspur, Nottingham Forest, Crystal Palace and Hull City.

How Decide Football reads it

Le Bris used the Zagreb defeat as a reason to change almost the whole team rather than tinker with it, and the fresh side gave the Stadium of Light the intensity he wanted. The test now is whether he goes back to his first-choice players or keeps faith with the side that beat Chelsea.
