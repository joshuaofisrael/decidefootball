Iraola: Gravenberch a day-to-day pain call before Crystal Palace

By Decide Football · 2026-11-07

Andoni Iraola has left Ryan Gravenberch's Premier League availability as a day-to-day pain decision ahead of Liverpool's trip to Crystal Palace.

The club posted his comments on X on 6 November 2026:

“Let's see if he can deal with the pain and if he can help us.”
Liverpool FC (@LFC), 6 November 2026

Liverpool's matching club write-up quotes Iraola saying Gravenberch is the only doubt and that the others will be the same as they have been. The midfielder missed the Champions League defeat by Fenerbahce with a heel injury and also sat out the preceding loss to Arsenal. Iraola called it a day-to-day matter of pain.

The decision in focus

This is a head coach's selection problem, not a vague fitness slogan. Iraola is publicly weighing whether Gravenberch can tolerate heel pain enough to help Liverpool at Selhurst Park. If the midfielder cannot, the XI changes; if he can, Iraola has signalled he wants him available.

That framing matters for Premier League minutes and for anyone projecting Liverpool's midfield three. Until the team sheet lands, the public logic is pain tolerance versus leaving him out.

How Decide Football reads it

Managers under injury doubt often hide behind “we'll see”. Iraola went further by naming Gravenberch as the lone question mark and by tying selection to pain management. Until results or the XI contradict him, that is the public decision rule for Sunday's Premier League fixture.
