Jorge Jesus brushes off Ronaldo question after Portugal's record 4-1 home defeat to Denmark

By Decide Football · 2026-11-15

Jorge Jesus has made it clear he is not looking back. Asked after Portugal's 4-1 Nations League defeat to Denmark in Braga on Saturday whether his side had missed Cristiano Ronaldo, the Portugal head coach replied, in a translation posted on X by Fabrizio Romano: "He's no longer on the national team, so what's missing?" He added that the only thing missing was Ronaldo's company.

A tribute night that went wrong

It was Portugal's first home match since Ronaldo announced his international retirement on 5 November. Sports Mole reports that the players walked out in his number seven shirt and fans unveiled a banner reading "Obrigado, Capitão". By full time some supporters had left and others were chanting Ronaldo's name. Citing the statistics site Zerozero, Sports Mole says Portugal had never before lost a competitive home match by three goals or more. It also describes the relationship between Jesus and Ronaldo as strained, with a disagreement over the forward's international role preceding his retirement.

Goal reports that Rasmus Højlund scored twice, with Pierre-Emile Højbjerg and Joachim Andersen also on target, before Bernardo Silva's late consolation. It was Jesus's first defeat in five matches in charge.

The decision ahead

Jesus now has to pick a side for Tuesday's trip to Wales in Cardiff, where Portugal must avoid defeat to win Group A4 and secure a seeded place in the quarter-finals, according to Goal. Rúben Dias and João Palhinha are suspended, which forces changes at the back and in midfield.

Our view: Jesus's answer closes the door on any debate about Ronaldo, but it also puts the spotlight squarely on his own choices. A strong response in Cardiff would end the questions quickly. Another poor night would invite more of them.
