Tuchel recalls Ben White to the England squad for the first time since March

By Decide Football · 2026-11-10

Thomas Tuchel has recalled Ben White to the England squad for November's UEFA Nations League fixtures, the Arsenal defender's first involvement since March, according to the FA's squad announcement.

The decision

White was ruled out of this summer's World Cup after an injury he sustained with Arsenal late last season, the FA said. Tuchel has now restored him to a 25 man group for the final two games of the Nations League group stage: Croatia at Wembley on Thursday 12 November and Spain in Madrid on Sunday 15 November, both kicking off at 7.45pm.

It is one of several returns in the squad. Djed Spence and Declan Rice are also back after missing the last camp through injury.

Why it matters for Tuchel

White gives the head coach an experienced defender who can play at right back or in the middle. The defensive group named alongside him includes Trent Alexander-Arnold, Jarrad Branthwaite, Lewis Hall, Ezri Konsa, Jarell Quansah and Djed Spence, so the recall widens the options rather than handing White an automatic place.

Defensive cover has already been tested in this window. Marc Guehi has withdrawn through injury, with Dan Burn called up in his place, and Bournemouth's James Hill has since received a first senior call up, the FA's squad updates show. The FA has also said Konsa will not be in contention for the Croatia game. Those changes make an experienced, versatile defender more valuable to Tuchel this week.

What is at stake

England are second in Group C. Avoiding defeat against Croatia would be enough to secure a place in next March's quarter finals, while finishing top would require two wins and a better goal difference than Spain.

What to watch

The question now is whether Tuchel trusts White straight away against Croatia or eases him back in. With the World Cup behind them and the next tournament cycle starting, how often he picks White over the coming windows will show whether this is a short term call or a return to the core of the squad.
