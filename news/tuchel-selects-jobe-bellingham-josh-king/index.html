Tuchel hands first England call ups to Jobe Bellingham and Josh King

By Decide Football · 2026-11-10

Thomas Tuchel has given first senior England call ups to two young midfielders, Jobe Bellingham of Borussia Dortmund and Fulham's Josh King, for the final UEFA Nations League group games against Croatia and Spain, the FA's squad announcement confirmed.

The decision

Both players step up from the men's under 21 squad. King already has some experience around the senior group after travelling to the United States in the summer for England's pre World Cup training camp in Florida. Bellingham joins his under 21 teammate after impressing with his form at Dortmund in the Bundesliga in the early part of this season, the FA said.

The pair are among nine midfielders in Tuchel's 25 man squad, alongside Elliot Anderson, Jude Bellingham, James Garner, Myles Lewis-Skelly and Declan Rice, who returns after missing the last camp through injury.

Why Tuchel might make the move now

The timing gives Tuchel a low risk look at the next generation. These are the last two games of the group stage, England are second in Group C and a draw against Croatia at Wembley would be enough to secure a quarter final place next March. Bringing young players into camp at this stage lets the coaching staff assess them in training without building the squad around them.

It also means Jobe Bellingham joins his brother Jude in the senior squad for the first time.

The bigger picture

The squad still leans on experience. Captain Harry Kane could become England's most capped men's player if he features, having equalled Peter Shilton's record of 125 caps in the last window. Picking Bellingham and King alongside that core suggests Tuchel is planning for the next tournament cycle while keeping continuity.

What to watch

A call up is not a cap. The fixtures are Croatia at Wembley on Thursday 12 November and Spain in Madrid on Sunday 15 November, both at 7.45pm. Whether either midfielder gets minutes, and in which game, will show how quickly Tuchel intends to integrate them.
