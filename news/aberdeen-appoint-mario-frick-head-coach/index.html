Aberdeen appoint former Luzern coach Mario Frick as head coach after Stephen Robinson's exit

By Decide Football · 2026-11-16

Aberdeen have appointed Mario Frick as head coach, the club announced on Sunday. The move is subject to UK visa formalities, and Frick brings assistant coach Roman Matter with him. Stephen Robinson left the club on 9 November.

Why the board chose him

Frick joins after a five-year spell at Luzern in the Swiss Super League. Aberdeen say he took over in December 2021 with the club bottom of the table, kept them up and later led them to fourth place and European qualification. Before that he won promotion to the Swiss top flight with Vaduz.

Chief executive Adrian Bevington said the appointment followed a thorough recruitment process run by himself and sporting director Lutz Pfannenstiel, and that the board was unanimous that Frick was the right choice. The club highlighted that Frick has given top-flight debuts to more than 30 academy players across almost 290 senior matches as a head coach.

As a player, Frick won 125 caps for Liechtenstein between 1993 and 2016 and scored a national record 16 goals.

The reaction

Supporters are split. BBC Sport published fans' views, ranging from welcome for an appointment from outside the usual pool of Scottish managers to worry about another change in the dugout.

What comes next

Aberdeen say Frick and Matter arrive at the start of this week to take training before next weekend's Scottish Premiership match away at Kilmarnock.

Our view: Frick's record of rescuing a bottom-placed side at Luzern is what Aberdeen are paying for. His lack of experience in Scottish football means early results will shape how long supporters give him.
