Bruno Fernandes Backs Carrick’s Call to Drop Him After Roma Comeback

By Decide Football · 2026-11-04

Michael Carrick's decision to drop his captain has been endorsed by the player himself. After Manchester United came from 2-0 down to beat Roma 3-2 in the UEFA Champions League league phase at Old Trafford on Tuesday night, Bruno Fernandes said his manager was right to put him on the bench last month, even if it was a punishment for his public criticism of United's tactics. BBC Sport reported it was the first time Fernandes had spoken to the media since being dropped.

“Bruno: Right to bench me after criticism of Man United tactics”
— ESPN FC (@ESPNsoccer), 3 November 2026

The decision

After United's previous Champions League match, a 4-1 defeat at Como, Fernandes blamed Carrick for the side's lack of pressing in a post-match interview, according to The Independent. Carrick responded by leaving him as an unused substitute for the next match, a 3-0 Premier League defeat at Bournemouth. Fernandes was restored to the side for the Premier League defeat by Chelsea, and against Roma he converted a twice-taken 58th-minute penalty that started the comeback before two goals from Matheus Cunha completed it, as The Guardian reported.

Why Fernandes says it was right

Speaking to Amazon Prime after the match, Fernandes said: “It's been tough, there's been a lot of speculation in the media. A lot of lies around many things that have happened between me and the manager, that we have cleared the air, then after that the manager took the decision to put me on the bench because I'm not performing. That's fair.”

He added: “Everyone thought that was a punishment for the words that I had. And, if it was, it was right. The manager is the one who makes the decisions. It doesn't change the belief I have of the manager and the staff and every player in this club.”

That matters for Carrick. A manager who drops his captain for speaking out risks losing the dressing room; instead, the captain has publicly backed the authority behind the call and responded with his best display of the season, according to ESPN.

Still under pressure

The win ended a run of nine matches without a victory, but it does not settle Carrick's position. United sit 17th in the Premier League with one win from their opening nine games, and BBC Sport reported that Carrick had been widely expected to be sacked after the Bournemouth defeat before the board decided he deserved a chance to turn things around. Carrick was given a two-year contract after winning 12 of his 17 games in charge last season.

Fernandes rejected the idea that the result was about one man. “We don't need to save anyone's job,” he said. “The club needed that win, the players needed that win.” Youri Tielemans was more direct when asked the same question: “That's a different question. The only thing we can do is stick behind him, fight for him and fight for the club.”

For now, the selection call that looked like a gamble a fortnight ago has the captain's public approval and a Champions League win attached to it. Whether it buys Carrick time depends on what follows in the Premier League.
